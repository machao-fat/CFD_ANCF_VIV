# MOVING_MESH_CFL_AND_MESH_QUALITY_ROOT_CAUSE_AUDIT_V1

## Decision

`NEXT_MOVING_MESH_SMOKE = NOT_AUTHORIZED`.

The corrected 0.1 s coupled runtime is immutable.  The first causal
instability is not a generalized-force mapping discrepancy: it is a
streamwise structural-motion/mesh-deformation escalation that produces
negative cells at the next fluid time layer.

## Parser and actual Courant timeline

The numerical-quality parser now accepts `Time = 0.05`, `Time = 0.05s`,
`Time = 0.05 s`, decimal-leading values, and scientific notation while
keeping a pending Courant record local to the following time record.  The
immutable logs reparse as 14, 16, and 14 records for slices 0, 1, and 2.

The printed `Courant Number` in this OpenFOAM 10 `pimpleFoam` path is computed
from `phi`, not directly from mesh velocity: `CourantNo.H` evaluates
`0.5*max(surfaceSum(abs(phi))/V)*deltaT`.  `pimpleFoam.C` prints it before
`mesh.update()` and would print the separate `mesh.phi()` metric only when
`checkMeshCourantNo` is enabled.  That option was not enabled, so a true Mesh
Courant number is unavailable in the failed CFD log.

Slice-0 Courant max was 0.188 at 0.025 s, 1.299 at 0.030 s, 7.081 at 0.055 s,
and 19.218 at 0.065 s. Slice-2 follows the same sequence (1.301, 7.098,
19.267).  Slice-1 was 13.647 at 0.065 s, then continued after the structure
participant had failed and reached `3.89e29` at 0.075 s and `2.39e56` at
0.080 s.  Those latter values are post-disconnect race evidence, not the
first cross-slice precursor.

## Causal ordering

The first conspicuous abnormal quantity is the received streamwise load at
the first coupled correction: each slice received `Fx = 2.58854e6 N`, while
the zero-time forces function output contained only `1683.78 N` viscous force.
The output is too sparsely written to reconstruct the t=0.005 forces-function
value, so this mismatch is recorded as a high-priority startup-load anomaly,
not asserted as a proven adapter bug.

The structural response follows directly: by prediction step 2, `ux` is about
`9.16e-3 m` with `vx` about `2.01 m/s`; by step 9, the single-step x jump is
about `0.118 m` and `vx` is about `25.6 m/s`; by step 13, slices 0/2 reach
`ux = 0.732/0.734 m` and `vx = 184 m/s`.  The motion values are internally
smooth at the Newmark time scale in the sense that position finite differences
have the same sign and magnitude as reported velocity; this is not a silent
pointDisplacement jump.  The transverse y component remains much smaller
(`max |y| <= 0.0114 m`).

Thus the sequence is:

1. abnormal streamwise fluid load at the first correction;
2. growing ANCF streamwise x velocity/displacement;
3. ALE/relative-flux Courant exceeds 0.5 at 0.030 s;
4. mesh-only replay develops negative cells at 0.070 s;
5. `pimpleFoam` FPE occurs while advancing that same next fluid layer.

## Mesh and displacement evidence

Initial mesh check: min volume `0.0015911`, max non-orthogonality `30.64`,
max skewness `0.503`, minimum face area `0.0015911`, and minimum edge length
`0.05598`; it passes.  At t=0.05 the coupled mesh still passes: min volume
`0.0016457`, non-orthogonality `30.81`, skewness `0.5031`, determinant
`0.4330`.

The displacement is total displacement, not an increment: OpenFOAM 10
`displacementLaplacianFvMotionSolver::curPoints()` forms `points0() +
pointDisplacement`.  At t=0.05 actual centroid, pointDisplacement, and
cellDisplacement agree with the explicit preceding-window command to within
`4.44e-13 m`.  This excludes double application at the cylinder and gives no
evidence of an old-time field-lifetime defect.  The full old-time internal
field history was not persisted, so it is correctly marked `NOT_AVAILABLE`
rather than inferred.

## Exact mesh-only replay

`moving_mesh_replay_v1_run_003` applied the persisted total 2-D displacement
history with the same `displacementLaplacian` mover, mesh and dt, but without
momentum or pressure equations. It reaches t=0.07 s.

At t=0.065 all three replay meshes pass. At t=0.070, slices 0 and 2 have 14
negative-volume cells each; their worst values are min volume
`-0.0015899/-0.0016214`, max non-orthogonality `176.29/175.35`, max skewness
`57.01/37.90`, and minimum face-volume ratios `-3.02/-3.70`. Slice 1 remains
healthy at t=0.070 because its x displacement is only `0.446 m`, compared
with about `0.732 m` for slices 0/2. The bad cells are generated by checkMesh
as `zeroVolumeCells` in the replay's 0.07 `polyMesh/sets`; their exact
cell-centre locations were not persisted by the original runtime and are not
claimed here.

This is direct evidence for `MESH_GEOMETRY_DEGRADATION`; it is sufficient to
exclude explicit-FSI instability as the first required explanation. An
additional prescribed-motion Navier–Stokes replay would not distinguish this
already demonstrated geometry failure, so it was not run.

## Root cause and minimal next repair

Classification: `STRUCTURAL_MOTION_SPIKE` plus `MESH_GEOMETRY_DEGRADATION`,
with `OPENFOAM_LOG_PARSER_BUG` repaired. The first unresolved upstream source
is the startup streamwise force anomaly. The extreme slice-1 Courant value is
not causal: it occurs only after its participant advanced beyond the failed
coupled time horizon.

The minimum next task is not a production smoke. It must diagnose and bound
the initial force impulse and its fluid initialization state (`U`, `p`, `phi`,
`Uf`, `meshPhi`, and time metadata) before the first force exchange, then add
per-step mesh-quality and mesh-Courant observability. Do not change dt,
PIMPLE, damping, mesh topology, or coupling scheme until that source is
proved.
